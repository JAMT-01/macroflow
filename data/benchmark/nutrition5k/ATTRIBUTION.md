# Nutrition5k sample attribution

The RGB and colorized depth images in this folder are unmodified samples from the official Nutrition5k dataset:

- `dish_1558459115`
- `dish_1558546663`
- `dish_1557862783`

Source: [google-research-datasets/Nutrition5k](https://github.com/google-research-datasets/Nutrition5k)

Paper: Quin Thames et al., "Nutrition5k: Towards Automatic Nutritional Understanding of Generic Food," CVPR 2021.

License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The images are bundled only as small, reproducible smoke-test cases. Nutrient and mass labels come from the dataset's official dish metadata.
